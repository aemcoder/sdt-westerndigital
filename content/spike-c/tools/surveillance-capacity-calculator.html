How does the calculator work?

This tool estimates total storage capacity based on the parameters you select, including the number of cameras, recording duration, resolution, frames per second (FPS), and compression format (MJPEG, H.264, H.265, or H.265+). It also factors in typical compression ratios, video quality, scene activity, and color depth — 30 bits for 4K and higher resolutions, and 16 bits for all other resolutions. Higher resolution, higher FPS, more cameras, and longer recording times all increase the storage required.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much storage do I need for CCTV?

Storage depends on the number of cameras, resolution, frames per second (FPS), compression, and recording duration. Higher video quality and longer recording times require more storage capacity.

How is CCTV storage calculated?

Storage is calculated based on how much video data your system generates. Factors such as resolution, FPS, compression format, recording time, and number of cameras all impact the total storage required. This estimate helps determine how much HDD storage is needed.

How long will 1TB record on CCTV?

Recording time depends on your system settings. Higher resolution, more cameras, and continuous recording will use storage faster, reducing how long footage can be stored.

What affects CCTV storage requirements?

Storage is affected by factors such as the number of cameras, resolution, FPS, compression format, video quality, scene activity, and recording duration.

How long do CCTV cameras keep footage?

Footage retention depends on available storage capacity and recording settings. Once storage is full, older footage may be overwritten depending on system configuration.

Can I use this calculator for NVR and IP camera systems?

Yes, this calculator can be used to estimate storage for NVR and IP camera systems by adjusting the number of cameras and video settings such as resolution and compression.

Are the results accurate?

Results are estimates and may vary depending on system configuration and environment.
