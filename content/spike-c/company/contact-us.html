Open Source Inquiries

We believe in open source and use it in many of our products. When we use open source in our software, we identify the open source we use in a Third-Party Notices section typically found in the help section. For our hardware products, the Third-Party Notices can be found in the accompanying software or in the product documentation. For open source licenses that require we provide the source code, you can find the source code for the relevant product on our support site. Unsolicited Submission

At Western Digital Corporation ("WD"), we have an ongoing program of research and development covering an assorted range of topics. Therefore, it is WD's policy not to review or consider ideas, suggestions, proposals, creative works or materials other than those that WD has specifically requested. The purpose of this policy is to help WD avoid future misunderstandings when new products, services and features developed internally by WD's employees might be similar or even identical to an idea or suggestion that may be submitted.
