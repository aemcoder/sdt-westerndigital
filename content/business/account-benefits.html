Frequently Asked Questions

I’m a small business. Do I qualify to join the WD for Business program?

Absolutely. In addition to larger enterprise businesses, we welcome SMBs (small- to medium-sized businesses), schools and universities, media organizations, professional creatives, and more to join and enjoy exclusive benefits. Whether you’re a reseller, integrator, or using our data solutions for your day-to-day business operations, we invite you tosign upand benefit from our business program today.

What is WD Credit?

In partnership with TreviPay, we are happy to offer a dedicated business line of credit through our WD Credit program. Learn more aboutWD Credit.

How do I apply for tax exemption for my business purchases?

Registered business members can request tax-exempt status. Once yousign upas a business member, you can submit your request from your account dashboard. In most cases, requests will be processed within 2-4 business days.
